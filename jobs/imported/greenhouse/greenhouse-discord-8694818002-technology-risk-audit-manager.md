&lt;div class="content-intro"&gt;&lt;p&gt;Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: &lt;strong&gt;play video games.&lt;/strong&gt; Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;/div&gt;&lt;p&gt;Discord's Internal Audit team exists to demonstrate effective risk management, process optimization, and adherence to relevant regulations — through a mix of independent assurance and advisory work that helps teams strengthen our overall control environment. This Technology Risk Audit Manager role owns the technical side of that mission — IT SOX/ITGC, system controls, and domains centered around consumer trust — that protect hundreds of millions of our users worldwide. You'll have the opportunity to help build our internal audit function from the ground up: shaping the frameworks and processes with an AI-native approach from day one, rather than bolting AI on after the fact. Your first few months will focus on learning Discord's financial-reporting systems landscape, understanding the company's GRC program structure, and evaluating AI-powered testing solutions — setting the foundation for a function that's built to scale as Discord grows toward enterprise readiness. This person will report to the Vice President of Internal Audit.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What You'll Be Doing&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Lead IT SOX/ITGC strategy and continuous improvement across financial-reporting-relevant systems&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Extend risk and controls assessment and assurance into consumer trust domains such as privacy, security, and trust &amp; safety&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Partner with the Engineering organization to ensure proper access controls, segregation of duties, change management, and CI/CD integrity are in place&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Guide control design through system implementations, migrations, and platform changes&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Manage teams and projects related to IT controls and technical audits, including external contractors and/or internal teammates&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Apply AI/automation tools to improve audit testing efficiency, anomaly detection, and control monitoring&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Track remediation, coordinate with external auditors, and report to senior leadership&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What you should have&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Accounting, or related field (or equivalent practical experience)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;8+ years in IT audit, risk management, or controls, spanning both financial (SOX/ITGC) and consumer trust domains (security, privacy, or trust &amp; safety a plus)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Deep ITGC fundamentals — access management, change management, computer operations, and SDLC controls&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;SOX/ICFR knowledge and independent risk assessment methodology — able to scope an audit, identify key risks, and design test procedures rather than execute a checklist&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Framework fluency across COSO, COBIT, and NIST CSF, applied appropriately by context&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Proven external audit/co-source coordination experience, with a track record of driving remediation action plans through to closure&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Hands-on experience applying AI or automation tools to audit processes, and strong communication skills translating technical risk for non-technical stakeholders&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Bonus points&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Consumer facing platform technology risk experience&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Subscription and/or Ad Tech experience&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience auditing homegrown systems and/or tools&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;AI governance experience&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Third-party/vendor risk depth&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Data privacy regulatory fluency&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;DevSecOps / CI-CD pipeline controls&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;CISA, CISSP, or CPA credential&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
