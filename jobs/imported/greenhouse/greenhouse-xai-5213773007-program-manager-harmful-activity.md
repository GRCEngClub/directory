They should be able to concisely and accurately share knowledge with their teammates.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;/div&gt;&lt;h3&gt;ABOUT THE ROLE:&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We serve our community of users and customers by working tirelessly to preserve free expression and choice, create limitless interactivity, and maintain a safe digital marketplace. The Safety Integrity Program Manager, Harmful Activity will drive priorities, measures, and system health for harmful activity, owning program standards and execution across upstream systems and downstream operations.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You are an experienced program manager with a strong background in content integrity, abuse, or risk programs at scale. You are skilled at setting requirements for detection and automation, translating policy and legal constraints into practical workflows and controls, and driving cross-functional partners to improve coverage, precision, and enforcement quality. You thrive in fast-paced environments and excel at turning operational signals into lasting improvements.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;RESPONSIBILITIES:&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Drive priorities, measures, and system health for harmful activity, and continuously improve execution&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Set requirements and success criteria for detection and automation, and drive Product, Engineering, and Operations to improve coverage, precision, and enforcement quality at scale.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Translate legal, policy, and technical constraints into practical requirements, workflows, controls, and escalation paths for Product, Engineering, and Operations.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Identify gaps in the current framework and drive enhancements to policies, procedures, controls, and workflows.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Own escalations, quality assurance, and complex case patterns for residual volume, and use those signals to harden standards and automation over time.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Align and drive Product, Engineering, Policy, Legal, and Operations partners to resolve dependencies, make decisions, and move critical work forward with clear accountable ownership.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Build and maintain the documentation, guidance, and operating standards that embed consistent execution across the program.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Monitor relevant regulatory developments, assess impact on the program, and ensure requirements stay accurate and executable.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Track and report operational performance, risks, and improvement opportunities with clear recommendations for leadership.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
