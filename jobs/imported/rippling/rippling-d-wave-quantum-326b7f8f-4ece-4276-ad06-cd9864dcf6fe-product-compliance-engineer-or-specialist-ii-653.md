D-Wave Quantum Inc. (NASDAQ: QBTS) is a leader in the development and delivery of quantum computing systems, software, and services. It is the world’s first commercial supplier of quantum computers, and the first and only to offer dual-platform quantum computing products and services, spanning both annealing and gate-model quantum computing technologies. D-Wave’s mission is to help customers realize the value of quantum today through enterprise-grade systems available on-premises and via its Leap™ quantum cloud service, which offers 99.9% availability and uptime. More than 100 organizations across commercial, government, and research sectors trust D-Wave to address complex computational challenges using quantum computing. Learn more about realizing the value of quantum computing today and how D-Wave is shaping the quantum-driven industrial and societal advancements of tomorrow: www.dwavequantum.com (http://www.dwavequantum.com/) .

About the role D-Wave is seeking a Product Compliance Engineer or Specialist II to support the development and certification of our quantum computing systems and related hardware. This role helps ensure products meet applicable safety, electromagnetic compatibility, environmental, and market-access requirements throughout the product lifecycle. Working within Hardware Engineering, you will collaborate with electrical, mechanical, cryogenic, systems, production, supply chain, service, and other cross-functional teams to translate regulatory and certification requirements into product-level activities, support testing and certification, address compliance issues, and maintain evidence of conformity. What you'll do - Participate in product and system design reviews to identify compliance risks early in the development lifecycle. - Support compliance assessments, testing, certification activities, and technical documentation for new and existing products. - Identify and interpret applicable product safety, regulatory, and industry standards with guidance from senior team members. - Work with engineering teams to identify and address compliance requirements during product development and changes. - Coordinate pre-compliance and formal testing with internal teams, external laboratories, and certification bodies. - Maintain compliance documentation, test reports, certifications, and technical records. - Investigate compliance issues and work with cross-functional teams to identify solutions. - Track changes to applicable standards and requirements and communicate relevant impacts to the team. - Contribute to the development and continuous improvement of compliance procedures, templates, checklists, and documentation practices. - Support the administration and continuous improvement of D-Wave’s EGBC Professional Practice Management Plan (PPMP), including documentation, training, compliance monitoring, audits, and corrective actions. What you'll bring - Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or a related technical field, or equivalent combination of education and experience. - 3+ years of experience in product compliance, regulatory, testing, quality, or a related engineering field. - Experience applying product safety, EMC/EMI, environmental, or related regulatory requirements to hardware products. - Working knowledge of UL, CSA, IEC, or EN standards; FCC or ISED requirements; or CE-marking requirements under applicable EU legislation. - Experience coordinating testing or certification activities with external laboratories or certification bodies. - Experience with technical documentation, compliance databases, or product certification processes. - Current or eligible registration as an Engineer-in-Training (EIT) or Professional Engineer (P.Eng.) with EGBC. - Good understanding of engineering fundamentals and product safety/compliance concepts. - Ability to interpret technical standards and requirements and apply them to products. - Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work through new or moderately complex issues. - Strong organizational skills and attention to detail. - Ability to work independently on defined tasks while seeking guidance when needed. - Strong written and verbal communication skills and ability to collaborate across teams. Bonus points for - Experience with complex electrical, electromechanical, scientific, cryogenic, vacuum, semiconductor, or industrial equipment. - Experience leading defined product-certification or compliance work packages. - Experience preparing CE technical documentation, product risk assessments, hazard analyses, or compliance requirements matrices. - Familiarity with RoHS, REACH, WEEE, or supplier material-declaration requirements. - A firm understanding of standards such as UL, CSA, IEC, FCC/ISED, CE, or related requirements. - Experience with technical documentation, compliance databases, or product certification processes. - Experience with engineering change control, configuration management, PLM, or quality-management systems.
