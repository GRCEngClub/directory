About Workstreet At Workstreet , we’re on an exciting journey to help businesses scale securely by designing and implementing cutting-edge security and compliance programs. As a fast-growing startup, we specialize in a wide range of GRC (governance, risk, and compliance) services that support frameworks across SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, CMMC, NIST 800-171, NIST 800-53, and FedRAMP. We empower companies to meet regulatory requirements and enhance their cybersecurity posture from day one.

Get to know the Special Programs Team Our Special Programs team runs Workstreet's signature 30-day engagement: a focused, limited-scope program that helps clients establish the foundation of their compliance program. For many companies, the VIP engagement is their first direct experience with Workstreet, so we treat every kickoff, working session, and deliverable as a first impression. We work with hundreds of companies across industries, move fast using common templates and methodologies, and act as the bridge between a client's security and sales points of contact. VIP Team members also listen for and advise clients on Workstreet's broader GRC and security services, setting the stage for each client's longer-term journey with us. The Opportunity We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented GRC Engineer I to join our onshore Special Programs team. This is an introductory GRC role designed for candidates with a cybersecurity background who are ready to apply that foundation to compliance frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR. You will deliver 30-day Special Programs engagements end to end under senior guidance: leading client calls and working sessions, completing core compliance milestones in Vanta, and leaving each client with a clear, working foundation for their program. You will work with client contacts during U.S. Eastern Time business hours, managing several concurrent engagements on a fixed 30-day clock. What you'll do - Lead VIP client calls and working sessions by running kickoffs, recurring check-ins, and working sessions; walking clients through their policies, control requirements, and evidence requests; and documenting decisions, action items, and follow-ups after every call. - Deliver 30-day VIP engagements on schedule, tracking milestones across several concurrent clients and keeping each engagement on pace for completion within the 30-day window. - Create custom client Policies, conduct Risk Assessments, and provide guidance to clients seeking compliance with various security frameworks. - Lay the foundation for client compliance programs aligned to SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, and other regulatory standards. - Engage directly with client contacts via multi-channel pathways (meetings, email, and shared channels) to collect inputs, clarify requirements, and deliver transparent status updates. - Create a strong first impression of Workstreet, delivering a polished, responsive client experience that reflects the standard clients can expect in a longer-term engagement. - Identify opportunities for continued engagement, listening for client needs beyond the VIP scope and partnering with internal sales and GRC teams on a smooth handoff. - Absorb technical mentorship from senior practitioners to rapidly improve VIP playbooks, compliance templates, and delivery velocity.
