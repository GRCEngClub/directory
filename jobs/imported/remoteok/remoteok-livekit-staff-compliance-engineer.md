## About This Role

Enterprise customers trust us with real-time audio, video and data belonging to their own end users, and before they build on LiveKit they want proof that we protect it. Our compliance program gives them that proof, and enterprise deals close on it. We are hiring a Compliance Engineer to lead the customer-facing side of that program. You will be the strategic partner to our sales team on security and compliance in enterprise deals, and you will own how we answer customers: security questionnaires, our answer library and the security terms in customer contracts.

You will join a small Trust team that covers security, IT and compliance, and you will own real parts of the program from your first week. You will share the day-to-day running of the compliance program with the rest of our Compliance team. We want someone who works themselves out of a job on the repeatable work. You will automate and simplify what you own, so your time goes to the larger deals and our compliance work grows more slowly than our customer base. We run SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS and HIPAA today, with ISO 9001 in progress. This role is remote, based in the United States or Canada.
