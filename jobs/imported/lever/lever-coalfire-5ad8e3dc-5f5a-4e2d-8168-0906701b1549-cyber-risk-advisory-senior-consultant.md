### Position Summary

The Cyber Risk Advisory (CRA) Senior Consultant leads day-to-day delivery of cybersecurity, privacy, AI, and information risk advisory engagements. Working with clients across industries, this role translates business objectives, regulatory obligations, and technical evidence into practical risk insights, prioritized recommendations, and actionable roadmaps. The Senior Consultant leads assessment workstreams, facilitates stakeholder interviews and workshops, and develops clear deliverables for executive and technical audiences. In close coordination with Project Managers, Directors, and delivery teams, this role manages scope, schedule, quality, and client expectations while coaching consultants and contributing to consistent delivery.

### What You'll Do

- Lead client discussions, interviews, and workshops with technical and non-technical stakeholders; communicate complex risk topics clearly and credibly

- Plan and execute cybersecurity, privacy, and compliance assessments, including evidence review, control testing, analysis, and validation of results

- Own assigned engagement workstreams from planning through closeout, managing milestones, deliverables, dependencies, and issues in coordination with project leadership

- Develop and quality-review reports that present risk and maturity findings, evidence-based observations, and prioritized, practical remediation recommendations

- Prepare and deliver executive briefings that connect industry threats and organizational exposure to clear decisions and actions

- Advise clients on the design, improvement, and operationalization of enterprise information security programs and related capabilities

- Evaluate cybersecurity and privacy policies, procedures, laws, regulations, and implementation directives across applicable industries and frameworks (e.g., NIST CSF, NIST SP 800-53, CIS Controls, CCPA, GDPR)

- Coach and support junior team members, contribute to knowledge sharing and practice development, and maintain relevant professional certifications and subject-matter expertise

### What You'll Bring

- At least 5 years of experience in cybersecurity, governance, risk and compliance (GRC), privacy, or a related field

- Consulting experience or relevant security experience in an industry segment (e.g., finance, healthcare, energy, or technology), with demonstrated responsibility for leading engagement workstreams

- Advanced English

- Demonstrated ability to independently manage assessment activities, client deliverables, competing priorities, and project timelines

- Strong working knowledge of current information risk, security, privacy, and AI trends, challenges, and solutions

- Practical experience applying information governance, risk, and security standards and frameworks (e.g., NIST, ISO, CIS) and relevant regulatory requirements

- Knowledge of information security solutions, tools, and utilities, and the ability to assess their role in a client’s control environment

- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent combination of education and experience

- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, sound professional judgment, and attention to detail

- Excellent written, verbal, presentation, client relationship, and time-management skills; ability to coach others and work independently

Bonus Points:

- CISSP, CISM, CCSK, CISA, CGEIT, CRISC, or other relevant certification(s)

- Cyber incident response experience

- AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform certification(s) and experience

- AI Risk and Governance experience
