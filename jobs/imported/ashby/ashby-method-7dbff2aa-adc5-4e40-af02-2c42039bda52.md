## Meet Method

Method has built the most modern way to connect to consumer financial accounts. Combining real-time liability connectivity with instant payment execution, Method’s API is designed to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. We have helped 45+ million users connect 350+ million liability accounts credential-less and processed over $2.5B in payments, helping users save millions in interest. One in every three credit cards in the United States is in the Method ecosystem and leading financial institutions like SoFi, Bilt, Cleo, Sezzle, Figure & Aven rely on our APIs to build magical experiences for millions of consumers. We’re a team of 50+ people spread across offices in Austin, SF, New York City, and Washington D.C! We’re excited to continue the momentum working alongside our investors and advisors from Andreessen Horowitz, Emergence Capital, Y Combinator, Avra, and Ardent. To learn more about us, check out our blog (https://methodfi.com/resources/company-updates)!

## The impact

Method’s largest customers are building the next generation of financial products. We’re hiring a Forward Deployed Product Manager to help shape what they build next and turn those ideas into meaningful growth for both their business and ours.

As part of Method’s Product team, you’ll be the dedicated product partner for a set of our most strategic accounts. You’ll work directly with senior product leaders to understand their ambitions, spot opportunities they haven’t considered, and bring a clear point of view on what’s possible with Method. You’ll bring them ideas of your own, develop theirs, and own the path from the first conversation through launch, bringing together Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success to make it happen.

This role combines product strategy, deep customer relationships, and the resourcefulness to get things built. You’ll test ideas with customers early, turn feedback into concrete next steps, and stay accountable for the outcome. As an early member of this team, you’ll also help define how Method partners with its largest customers to build new products and grow.

## What you'll do

- Own the strategic product relationship with Method's largest accounts, working with these customers to identify, then build, new initiatives and revenue opportunities.
- Collaborate directly with our customers’ CPOs and product leaders, building relationships where you are their first call for anything net-new involving Method.
- Orchestrate the full account team, pulling in Sales, Customer Success, Implementation, and Engineering at the right moments to bring these initiatives to life.
- Proactively put new ideas in front of customers early, learn from their response, and iterate quickly.
- Help define the playbook for how Method runs forward-deployed coverage as the model expands to more accounts.
- Work cross functionally to guide strategy on maximizing value from existing customer integrations

## Who you are

- You have a consulting, client services, customer success, solutions, or account management background, and you are at your best in front of senior customers.
- You have product instincts, either as a PM or through a track record of shaping what gets built from the customer side.
- You are technically fluent enough to talk APIs, data flows, and performance tradeoffs with engineers and product leaders.
- You stay with hard problems and do not need a perfect plan to get started.
- You default to action: when you have an idea, you test it with the customer instead of talking yourself out of it.
- You have taste, and you care about doing the work the right way.
- You can operate both reactively (customer has an idea you run with) and proactively (you chase customers to explore your new idea)
- You already use AI tools to move faster in your day-to-day work.

## Extra Awesome

- Experience at a fintech, lending, payments, or financial data company (e.g., Plaid, Stripe, Cash App, SoFi).
- Background at a top consulting firm before moving into an operator role (CS, bizops, solutions, or product for example)
- Exposure to lending, underwriting, or credit data.
