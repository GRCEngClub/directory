Senior Technical Project Manager (FedRamp)

Second Front Systems is looking for an experienced, accreditation-focused Senior Technical Project Manager to strengthen our federal delivery and compliance operations. Operating at the intersection of defense software deployment and federal security requirements, you will be responsible for driving the VA deployment pathway through FedRamp authorization and accreditation gates. You will coordinate security documentation, manage compliance timelines, and interface with VA authority representatives to meet accreditation milestones, ensuring that Second Front Systems achieves FedRamp authorization and operational deployment into VA environments on schedule. We need someone capable of navigating FedRamp security control requirements and federal assessment processes; translating technical security capabilities into compliance artifacts that satisfy authorizing officials; and managing competing priorities across engineering, security, and federal stakeholder demands. As a key partner to federal customers, internal product teams, and third-party assessment organizations, you will establish the operational pathway that enables Second Front Systems to serve the warfighter through authorized federal systems, serving as the mission lead who owns delivery accountability for federal compliance and deployment. Note: Candidates must reside in one of our approved hiring hubs: • DC/Maryland/Virginia • Denver/Colorado Springs, CO This is a full-time position. What You'll Do • Own program startup activities including WBS definition, business rhythm establishment, process definition, and infrastructure setup for contract compliance. • Develop and maintain integrated master schedules, milestones, and dependencies across multi-stakeholder programs. • Manage contract lifecycle activities including CDRL generation and delivery, modification tracking, and deliverable validation against contractual requirements. • Orchestrate recurring status meetings, customer reviews, program reviews, and executive briefings with clear updates on program health and priorities. • Monitor program financials including budget baselines, burn rates, EACs, and obligation timelines; flag variances and drive corrective actions. • Synchronize with engineering, product, and security teams to ensure technical deliverables align with program requirements, schedules, and compliance standards.

What You Bring • 15+ years of program management experience specifically within DoD acquisition and government contracting. • Active Top Secret/SCI clearance or demonstrated immediate eligibility; U.S. citizenship required. • Proven experience interfacing directly with senior government stakeholders and delivering executive-level briefings to DoD leadership. • Demonstrated mastery of contract management, CDRL generation, schedule development (IMS), WBS definition, and ROM support. • Expertise in integrated master schedule development, milestone tracking, and managing multi-stakeholder dependencies on complex government programs. • Proficiency in program financial tracking including budget baseline management, burn rate analysis, EAC forecasting, and obligation timeline management.
