# Who We Are

Notion is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vkpYpWfEK5s). We're building one place where your knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools live side by side, so work is faster, clearer, and less fragmented. Millions of individuals, small teams, and large companies run their work on Notion.

Notinos (our employees) are customer zero in bringing this future of work to life. We care about craft, building things that last, and the belief that great work is still fundamentally human. Our goal isn’t to ship the next feature. Each and every team of Notinos is working to set the standard for how humans work together in the AI era. From building a business’s system of record to making and managing AI agents to automating away the busy work, we care deeply about giving our customers more time for their life’s work.

### About The Role

As Manager, Forward Deployed Architect, Japan & Korea, you will lead a team that helps enterprise customers turn AI ambition into operational reality. You will manage and develop Forward Deployed Architects across Japan and Korea, helping customers build meaningful workflows and solutions while establishing the practices that enable consistent, high-quality delivery.

This is a player-coach role for a builder, seller, and people leader. Your primary impact will come through the performance and growth of your team, rather than through personally owning delivery. You will stay close to customers and engagements, working with account teams from presales through delivery, coaching your team, and contributing hands-on when needed. You will report to the Head of Services, Forward Deployed, APAC.

You will partner with Solutions Consultants and Outcome Architects to identify opportunities, shape engagements, and connect proposed solutions to customer outcomes. You will also manage staffing and delivery, support commercial and contractual processes, and build repeatable ways of working with internal teams and service partners.

You will partner with Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) managers on engagements that require engineering expertise. The FDA organization owns time-bound Services engagements, including discovery, solution architecture, implementation, launch readiness, enablement, outcome delivery, and handoff. FDE owns engineering-intensive implementation requiring production code or deep technical specialization. Together, you will establish clear engagement boundaries, staffing and escalation mechanisms, quality standards, and a unified customer experience.

This is an opportunity to help define what forward-deployed work looks like at Notion as we evolve into an Agent OS. You will help customers put new AI-powered workflows into practice, share what works with partners, and shape an emerging service in a fast-changing environment.

### What You'll Achieve

Customer delivery

- Guide the Japan and Korea portfolio from discovery and solution design through implementation, enablement, and handoff, keeping customer outcomes at the center of each engagement.
- Review delivery health, scope, budget, timelines, and risks; coach team members through challenges and support escalations and change requests.
- Stay close to strategic engagements and customer stakeholders, providing guidance and executive support when needed.
- Assign the right team members to engagements, establish co-delivery approaches, and provide hands-on implementation support when needed.
- Partner with Forward Deployed Engineers and other stakeholders when engagements require engineering expertise, with clear ownership and handoffs.
- Improve delivery quality and consistency through playbooks, templates, reference architectures, quality reviews, and lessons learned, developed and shared with teams and service partners.

Pre-sales and solution scoping

- Partner with Solutions Consultants, Outcome Architects, and account teams to identify customer needs and opportunities for paid and unpaid Services engagements.
- Lead or support discovery and shape proposals, defining desired business outcomes, solution approach, deliverables, implementation plans, timelines, and risks.
- Qualify and prioritize incoming opportunities against customer needs, commercial value, delivery risk, and available capacity. Make clear decisions to accept, defer, redirect, or decline work.
- Develop a target-account strategy and proactively drive joint Services proposals with Solutions Consultants during your first six months.
- Manage Services opportunities and forecasts, and support customer contracts, waivers, and change orders throughout the engagement lifecycle.
- Turn recurring customer needs into packaged solutions, and share field insights and lessons with regional peers, Product, Engineering, PMM, and Services leadership.

Team management and operations

- Hire, onboard, manage, and retain Forward Deployed Architects across Japan and Korea, setting clear expectations and managing performance against the FDA levels framework.
- Develop team members through engagement reviews, shadowing, work-product feedback, coaching, and regular career conversations.
- Turn delivery experience into practical guidance and training; set standards for discovery, solution architecture, implementation, engagement leadership, and customer enablement.
- Manage staffing and capacity across presales and delivery, matching work to skills and availability while protecting time for onboarding, coaching, and reusable assets.
- Build effective operating practices and run a regular cadence to review pipeline, demand, allocation, delivery health, and risks, adjusting priorities as needs change.
- Collaborate with FDA and FDE managers in other regions to share engagement updates, learning, and best practices.

### Skills You'll Need to Bring

- 5+ years of experience in professional services, solutions architecture, implementation, customer engineering, or a similar customer-facing technology role.
- Experience across the enterprise services lifecycle, from presales and discovery through proposals, contract management, and delivery. Strong commercial and delivery judgment, including estimation, margin awareness, risk, change control, and escalation.
- Experience managing and developing a team, including hiring, onboarding, feedback, performance management, career development, and team health.
- Ability to turn personal expertise into repeatable coaching, training, and standards that improve team performance, and experience developing packaged services or reusable delivery methods and assets.
- Experience managing staffing and capacity across concurrent engagements, and designing intake and prioritization processes that balance customer urgency, strategic value, delivery risk, and available capacity.
- Hands-on experience building and delivering technology or AI solutions, with tangible examples and a builder mindset. Technical fluency across areas such as AI workflows, enterprise systems, APIs, integrations, data, automation, and governance; production coding is not required, but you must be able to reason through tradeoffs and partner credibly with FDE.
- Strong customer-facing discovery, communication, problem-solving, and solution architecture skills, with the ability to review complex work, guide teams, and align stakeholders around outcomes.
- Fluency in Japanese and English, and the ability to work across teams and customers in Japan and Korea.
- Comfort navigating ambiguity, learning quickly, and adapting strategy and execution as the business evolves.
