JOB SUMMARY
Part of the IT Compliance team within Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC), Global Cybersecurity. Responsible for IT compliance assignments within the IT organization, including control testing and compliance monitoring of vulnerability management, CIS benchmarks, Operational Technology (OT) and software compliance. Actively participates in establishing and maintaining the IT Compliance Program, verifying that IT controls are designed and operating effectively against Jabil policies, standards, regulations and business objectives, and providing ongoing assurance through control testing, evidence and continuous improvement.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

- Actively participate in the IT Compliance Program across its full scope, including control testing, compliance monitoring, support of SOX ITGC, audit support, exceptions, findings and reporting

- Build broad knowledge of IT controls, compliance processes and technologies, and cross-train with team members to provide flexible coverage across the program rather than working in a single domain

- Perform control testing to assess the design and operating effectiveness of IT controls, including Jabil general and application controls, across corporate and site; document results and evidence, log control gaps as findings, and report control effectiveness to Governance and Risk

- Support the automation of control testing and compliance monitoring by defining test logic, pass/fail criteria and data requirements, working with technical and platform owners to source system-generated evidence, and validating automated results for accuracy and completeness

- Support compliance activities for vulnerability management, CIS benchmarks, Operational Technology (OT) and software compliance, tracking remediation and monitoring compliance against defined standards and SLAs

- For non-compliance or ineffective controls, guide and facilitate IT Exception submissions, ensuring appropriate documentation aligned with policies and standards

- Highlight compliance risks identified through control testing and audits, and recommend and coordinate the tracking of corrective actions to ensure closure of remediation items and control gaps

- Support the development, automation and maintenance of compliance reporting, including dashboards and metrics on compliance status and control effectiveness

- Act as a liaison between IT and internal/external audit teams to support audit & remediation activities

- Serve as a focal point for escalation and resolution of compliance issues

- Train, support and educate the IT organization on regulatory and policy requirements, including Jabil general and application controls

- Use AI and standardized team practices (e.g., shared prompts, templates) to improve the consistency and efficiency of compliance work, and contribute improvements back to the team

- Drive continuous improvement of processes, procedures, and systems, ensuring they are efficient and not redundant

- Maintain positive relationships with all employees and seek suggestions for improving compliance of IT processes and systems

- Participate in and support IT Compliance initiatives and ensure timely delivery; organize work effectively and efficiently

- Maintain discretion and confidentiality in all areas pertaining to data and proprietary information whether internal to Jabil or customer-specific

- Participate in the exchange of ideas within the department. Ask questions and encourage input from other team members

- Provide regular updates to appropriate managers and consult with them on complex or unusual situations

- Comply with company security policies and procedures

- May perform other duties and responsibilities as assigned

JOB QUALIFICATIONS & KNOWLEDGE REQUIREMENTS

- Bachelor's Degree. Prefer a degree in a technical field such as Information Security, Management Information Systems or Computer Science.

- Experience in IT compliance, IT audit or security operations; experience supporting vulnerability management, CIS benchmark, Operational Technology (OT) and software compliance is highly desired.

- Analyze technical data and present clear compliance status to technical and non-technical audiences.

- Working knowledge of ISO 27001/27002, NIST CSF 2.0 and SOX ITGC is preferred.

- Exposure to OT security (e.g., ISA/IEC 62443), software asset compliance or AI governance (e.g., ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF) is an added advantage.

- Strong written and verbal communication; able to drive action from owners without direct authority and coordinate across global, remote teams.

- Meticulous attention to detail with the ability to perform tasks accurately and precisely.

- Eager to learn new knowledge and skills; able to manage changing priorities and deadlines.

- Experience with GRC tools (e.g., ServiceNow, OneTrust) is preferred.

- Professional certification preferred: CISA, CISSP, CRISC or CompTIA Security+.

- Proficiency in the use of personal computers, Microsoft Office products (Excel, Word and PowerPoint) and email skills required.

- PowerBI Knowledge is an added advantage.
