Each day, we verify more than 3 million identities, flagging 150k+ fraud attempts in the process. The problems we work on are challenging, and solving them takes deep domain expertise, rigorous analysis, and a willingness to dig into the details.
At SentiLink, you'll work alongside smart, highly collaborative colleagues who respect each other's time and judgment, take ownership, and follow through on their commitments. Together, we do truly meaningful work protecting the identities of innocent consumers and flagging the fraudsters attempting to exploit them.
We're well capitalized and growing quickly. We already serve 13 of the top 15 U.S. banks, we're expanding into new markets, and we're backed by Craft Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz, NYCA, and Max Levchin. We've been named to the Forbes Fintech 50 and as a 2026 FICO Industry Vanguard Decision Award Winner. We were also the first company to go live with the electronic Consent Based Social Security Number Verification (eCBSV) service, and we've testified before the U.S. Congress on the future of identity.
We want SentiLink to be the best place you've ever worked. We offer generous benefits and support a range of working arrangements, from fully remote to in-office. We are a digital-first company with strong collaboration across the U.S. and India, and we meet in person regularly to build relationships. We have offices in Austin, San Francisco, New York City, Seattle (Bellevue), Los Angeles, and Chicago in the U.S., and in Gurugram (Delhi) and Bengaluru in India.
Role:
SentiLink's Information Security team protects our systems, data, and customers while enabling the business to move quickly and securely. We operate a modern, AWS-based environment and support security and compliance programs including FedRAMP, SOC 2, and PCI DSS.
We're looking for a Senior Cloud Security Engineer to own and improve the technical security implementation of SentiLink's AWS GovCloud environment. This is a hands-on engineering role focused on building, maintaining, and operating security controls—not a compliance or audit-management position.
You'll work across AWS infrastructure, Kubernetes, identity and access management, security tooling, vulnerability management, and monitoring. You will partner closely with Infrastructure, Security Engineering, IT, and our Security & Risk team to turn security and FedRAMP requirements into effective, maintainable implementations. You should be comfortable independently identifying gaps, designing pragmatic solutions, and following through on implementation and ongoing operation.
This is an individual contributor role with meaningful technical ownership and no people-management responsibilities.
This is a U.S.-based role. U.S. citizenship is required to meet applicable government customer and access requirements for the position. Candidates must also be able to satisfy applicable agency personnel-screening and system-access requirements.

Responsibilities:

- Design, implement, maintain, and operate security controls within SentiLink's AWS GovCloud environment, including controls supporting FedRAMP Moderate requirements and continuous monitoring.

- Engineer and improve cloud security across AWS accounts, IAM, networking, EKS/Kubernetes, Linux workloads, secrets management, logging, and encryption.

- Define and implement role-based access control and least-privilege access patterns for teams, services, and administrators; partner with IT on identity lifecycle and access provisioning.

- Develop, deploy, and maintain cloud infrastructure and security automation using Terraform and GitOps/CI/CD workflows.

- Own or provide durable technical coverage for security services in GovCloud, including vulnerability scanning and management integrations, dynamic application security testing, security telemetry, and related tooling.

- Investigate security findings, assess real-world risk, coordinate remediation with the engineering teams that own affected systems, and implement fixes directly for security-owned infrastructure and controls.

- Improve vulnerability-management operations and technical evidence collection, ensuring findings can be accurately tracked, validated, and reported through FedRAMP processes.

- Identify gaps in security visibility, monitoring, incident-response readiness, and operational processes; build and implement practical improvements rather than simply maintaining existing controls.

- Support security incidents, response exercises, technical assessments, and remediation of audit or penetration-testing findings affecting the government environment.

- Establish clear operational ownership, documentation, runbooks, and backup coverage for critical GovCloud security systems; participate in the security on-call rotation.

- Collaborate with the Security & Risk team, which owns FedRAMP governance, audit coordination, and POA&M oversight, while Security Engineering owns technical implementation and operational effectiveness.

Requirements:

- 5+ years of hands-on experience in cloud security engineering, infrastructure security, platform engineering, or a closely related technical role.

- Strong practical experience designing and securing production systems on AWS, including IAM, VPC networking, logging/monitoring, and security services.

- Experience deploying and operating cloud infrastructure using Terraform or equivalent infrastructure-as-code tools.

- Working knowledge of Kubernetes/container security, Linux security, CI/CD, and modern infrastructure operations.

- Demonstrated ability to implement and operate technical security controls rather than only assess compliance or provide recommendations.

- Experience with vulnerability assessment, remediation coordination, security findings, and continuous improvement of security operations.

- Ability to troubleshoot complex infrastructure and security issues, independently drive projects to completion, and produce clear technical documentation.

- Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate with Infrastructure, Engineering, IT, and security compliance stakeholders.

- Understanding of security principles and frameworks such as NIST SP 800-53, FedRAMP, SOC 2, or comparable standards, with the ability to connect control requirements to real technical implementations.

- U.S. citizenship and ability to satisfy applicable government customer access and personnel-screening requirements.

Nice to have:

- Direct experience operating or securing AWS GovCloud systems within a FedRAMP Moderate or comparable government-authorized environment.

- Experience implementing NIST SP 800-53 technical controls, supporting continuous monitoring, and producing technical evidence for assessors.

- Experience with AWS Organizations, SCPs, IAM Identity Center, EKS, Kubernetes security tooling, and multi-account cloud environments.

- Experience operating vulnerability scanners, DAST tooling, cloud posture management, and security logging/detection platforms.

- Programming or scripting experience in Python, Go, Bash, or similar languages to develop useful security automation.

- Experience with government cloud access models, privileged access, just-in-time access, and identity lifecycle processes.

- Experience in fintech, identity verification, financial services, or another highly regulated SaaS environment.
