CX2 is a next-generation defense technology company securing spectrum dominance for the United States and its allies. CX2 builds AI-enabled hardware and software platforms to detect, disrupt, and defend the electromagnetic spectrum across land, air, sea, and space. Headquartered in El Segundo, California, CX2 develops technology designed to give warfighters the ability to see, understand, and act across the invisible battlefield.

We’re looking for a sharp, pragmatic, and detail-oriented Contracting & Compliance Lead to help us navigate the complex legal, regulatory, and contractual landscape of working with the U.S. Department of Defense. This role will manage government and commercial contracts, ensure compliance with federal acquisition regulations (FAR/DFARS), and help build the internal processes that allow us to move fast while staying secure and compliant. You’ll work cross-functionally with leadership, engineering, legal counsel, and program teams to ensure we are meeting our obligations and protecting the company’s interests as we scale into larger and more complex defense programs.

This role will be based onsite at our Company HQ in El Segundo, CA.  A remote working relationship is not available for this position.
Overall Responsibilities

- Manage the end-to-end contract lifecycle, including proposal review, contract negotiation, execution, and compliance monitoring.

- Act as the primary internal point of contact for all DoD contract matters—ensuring we meet deliverables, terms, and reporting requirements.

- Interpret and implement FAR, DFARS, and agency-specific clauses, and advise internal stakeholders accordingly.

- Track flow-down requirements from government customers; ensure these are reflected in subcontracts and internal practices.

- Work with outside counsel and executive leadership to assess risk and structure favorable terms on high-value or strategic contracts.

- Help establish and maintain compliance programs across areas like export control (ITAR/EAR), cybersecurity (NIST 800-171 / CMMC), and classified programs.

- Support internal and external audits, government reviews, and due diligence processes.

- Draft, maintain, and enforce contracting and compliance policies, templates, and workflows as we scale.

Required Skills

- Minimum 5–10 years of experience in defense contracting, legal, or compliance roles—ideally at a defense contractor, federally funded startup, or government agency.

- Deep working knowledge of FAR, DFARS, ITAR, and other regulatory frameworks relevant to U.S. defense contracting.

- Experience managing the full lifecycle of DoD contracts, including fixed-price, cost-plus, and OTA agreements.

- Highly organized, process-oriented, and capable of balancing compliance with speed in a startup environment.

- Strong communication skills and the ability to translate complex regulatory language into clear operational guidance.

- U.S. citizenship and ability to obtain and maintain a security clearance.

ITAR Restrictions

- To Conform to US Government Regulations, applicant must be a U.S. Citizen or National or U.S. lawful, permanent resident (aka green card holder), or be eligible to obtain the required authorizations from the U.S. Department of State. Note: Those currently on OPTs and/or H1Bs do not currently qualify for ITAR. Learn more about the ITAR here.

Bonus Points

- Experience implementing NIST 800-171 and managing ongoing CMMC requirements.

- Familiarity with classified contract management, DD254s, or FCL processes.

- Legal training (JD or paralegal certification), or prior work as a contracts officer, contracting specialist, or compliance lead in a defense context.
