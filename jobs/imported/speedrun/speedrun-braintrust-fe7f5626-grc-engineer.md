About the company
Braintrust is the agent observability platform. By actively applying intelligence to agent traces and automatically surfacing the most critical patterns, Braintrust gives teams the visibility to understand how agents behave in production and the tools to improve them.

Teams at Notion, Stripe, Box, OpenAI, and Cloudflare use Braintrust to trace their agents, find the issues in their observability data, and run evals that tell them how to improve.

About the role
Braintrust’s customers rely on the trustworthiness of our people, processes, and code. As a GRC Engineer on the Braintrust security team, you’ll design and build the controls that scale our customer assurance operations to meet the stringent requirements of the most security-conscious companies. You’ll make audits low-lift, partner with product engineering to hit compliance benchmarks, supply our GTM team with top-notch security collateral, and build an innovative GRC program in an agent-forward environment.

What you'll do

- Define and enforce sensible security controls that work for us, our customers, and our auditors (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)

- Build agents, integrations, pipelines, and whatever else is needed to make customer assurance operations (e.g. security questionnaires) high quality and low effort

- Manage knowledge - from source code to docs, contracts to CRM, if the answer exists, you know how to find it

- Work directly with customers - listen, understand what they need, and give them high-fidelity information that builds trust

- Operate the risk roadmap by understanding what matters, building consensus on prioritization, and communicating the ROI on security team efforts

- Keep the security program’s heartbeat with process - tabletops, internal audits, and red team exercises build our capacity to weather the vulnpocalypse

- Partner with Legal to support privacy operations and represent Braintrust as a responsible steward of personal data

Ideal candidate credentials

- Comfortable using code to solve problems

- Knowledgeable about modern cloud and endpoint technology stacks

- Adept AI user who understands when and how to use AI for a task

- Experience supporting revenue teams selling to security-sensitive organizations

- Deeply comfortable letting compliance be an end product of good security

- Creative when tackling difficult requirements and tough tradeoffs

- Strong written and verbal communication
