Assurity Trusted Solutions (ATS) is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Government Technology Agency (GovTech). As a Trusted Partner over the last decade. ATS offers a comprehensive suite of products and services ranging from infrastructure and operational services, governance and assurance services as well as managed processes. In a dynamic digital & cyber landscape where trust & collaboration is key, ATS continues to drive mutually beneficial business outcomes through collaboration with GovTech, government agencies and commercial partners to mitigate cyber risks and bolster security postures.

Responsibilities:

- Design and operate AI-assisted automation for System Security Plan(SSP) review, evidence classification, control mapping, compliance monitoring and security-posture reporting. Define appropriate human review points and validate the reliability of automated outputs.
- Write, review and maintain practical security policies, standards and implementation guidance. Policies must translate organisational intent into clear, technically meaningful and enforceable requirements that product and engineering teams can apply.
- Own and improve the CISO Office’s governanceand compliance monitoring approach, including the definition of what good security control implementation looks like for products within scope.
- Strengthen SSP verification by translating broad or process-oriented clauses into specific, testable control statements and evidence expectations that product teams can understand and apply.
- Coordinate Information and Communications Technology (ICT) risk management activities, including risk identification, treatment tracking, escalation, acceptance and formal closure, with clear ownership and due dates.
- Develop and maintain security posture reporting and the CISO dashboard, ensuring that reported status is supported by current evidence and highlights material gaps, trends and overdue actions.
- Establish repeatable timelines and evidence requirements for audit follow-up, compliance reviews, lower-level checks and other governance activities arising from previous assessments.
- Support the rollout and operationalisation of policies for AI-assisted coding, sensitive or high-impact data handling, software as a service (SaaS) usage, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, logging and related emerging technology risks.
- Work with security and engineering teams to identify opportunities for compliance monitoring, workflow automation and agent-assisted evidence review while retaining human judgement for risk decisions and closure.
- Coordinate governance forums and working sessions with product teams and central stakeholders, ensuring decisions, actions, exceptions and dependencies are recorded and followed through.
- Maintain reusable governance guidance, control interpretations, evidence examples and lessons learned to improve consistency across future SSP and risk reviews.
- Provide concise, decision-ready advice to CISO leadership on systemic control gaps, resource constraints, residual risk and priorities for remediation.

Requirements

- Security frameworks or standards such as ISO 27001, NIST CSF, NIST SP 800-53, CSA CCM or equivalent control frameworks.
- Governance tooling, GRC platforms, evidence repositories, dashboards or workflow automation.
- Cloud and software-as-a-service (SaaS) security controls, identity and access management, logging, data protection and software-development lifecycle practices.
- Relevant professional certifications such as CISSP, CISA, CRISC, ISO 27001 Lead Implementer or Lead Auditor. Certifications are useful but do not replace practical judgement.

Experience

- Technical security experience in cloud, application, infrastructure, platform, development, security and operations (DevSecOps), security architecture, security engineering or a closely related area, combined with experience in governance, risk or compliance.
- Strong understanding of how technical controls operate in practice, including identity and access management, logging and monitoring, vulnerability management, secure software delivery, cloud security and data protection.
- Ability to write clear, practical and enforceable control requirements, guidance and management papers for technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Experience coordinating reviews across multiple products, platforms or business units, with the judgement to distinguish administrative completion from meaningful control effectiveness.
- Comfort working directly with cloud, software as a service (SaaS), software delivery and artificial intelligence (AI)-related risks. Experience with System Security Plans, threat modelling, AI governance, development, security and operations (DevSecOps), or automated compliance monitoring is advantageous.
- Strong stakeholder-management, facilitation and written-communication skills, with the ability to challenge constructively and drive actions to closure.
