Optasia is a real-time financial intelligence company. We build precision intelligence that unlocks financial opportunity at scale, running millions of automated credit decisions every day across high-growth markets where formal credit data is limited. Our AI-powered platform turns permitted behavioural data and real-time signals into precise, responsible credit decisions. Embedded within the systems of telcos, banks, digital wallets and other partners, it helps them extend relevant financial services to more people while maintaining strong portfolio control and opens profitable new segments that were previously out of reach. This is how Optasia turns potential into financial progress, responsibly and at scale.

Central Africa/West Africa is one of the most dynamic regulatory environments for digital finance on the continent. The region is actively shaping how payment services, microfinance and digital lending are licensed and supervised, and national authorities are moving quickly on data protection and consumer protection. The rules being written now will determine how financial inclusion grows across the region for the next decade.

As our Regional Regulatory Affairs & Compliance Specialist for this region, you will be Optasia's voice with regulators in the region and the person who makes sure we meet every obligation that applies to us. You will combine two disciplines in one role: regulatory affairs (engaging regulators, anticipating change and helping shape it) and regulatory compliance (making sure our operations meet the rules today). You will also be a trusted partner to our commercial teams, helping them launch and grow services the right way.

What you will do

Regulatory affairs: engage, anticipate and influence

- Own regulator relationships. Act as Optasia's first point of contact with regulators and supervisory bodies, including BCEAO, COBAC, ministries of finance, financial intelligence units, data protection authorities and telecoms regulators. Build trusted, long-term relationships, not just attend meetings.
- Run horizon scanning for the region. Track new and proposed laws, regulations, instructions and prudential guidelines, assess what they mean for our business, and give impacted teams early warning with a clear recommendation on what to do.
- Shape the conversation. Prepare position papers and consultation responses, and represent Optasia in industry associations, fintech forums and regulatory sandboxes where appropriate.
- Support licensing and authorisations. Help determine which licences or approvals apply to each of our services, and support applications, renewals and regulator follow-up together with our partner banks and MNOs.

Regulatory compliance: know our obligations and meet them

- Keep the obligations register current. Maintain an up-to-date view of every regulatory obligation that applies to Optasia in each market, including reporting deadlines, licence conditions and prudential requirements, and make sure each one has an owner.
- Document the regulatory basis of every deployment. For each service we run, record which licence and legal framework it operates under, and flag gaps before they become problems.
- Assess and monitor compliance. Working with the Regulatory Compliance Managers, run structured compliance assessments, measure our regulatory risk exposure and track remediation to closure.
- Manage regulatory reporting and requests. Coordinate accurate, on-time responses to regulator requests, inspections and periodic reporting, and partner with Internal Audit and other assurance functions under our combined assurance model.
- Build a compliance culture. Design and deliver training and awareness sessions so that colleagues across the business understand the rules that apply in the region and why they matter.

Business partnership: help us grow the right way

- Enable go-to-market. Advise local commercial, presales and deployment teams on regulatory requirements from the first conversation with a partner through to launch, so that new services launch on a sound regulatory footing.
- Answer partner and customer questions. Respond to regulatory and compliance queries from MNOs, banks and other partners, including due diligence questionnaires, supplier onboarding and contractual reporting obligations.
- Contribute to the wider strategy. Bring a CEMAC/WEAMU perspective to the group's Regulatory Affairs & Compliance strategy and share lessons with colleagues covering other regions.

What success looks like in your first year

- You have working relationships with key regulators in priority markets, and they know who to call at Optasia.
- A complete, current obligations register for the region, with every deployment's regulatory basis documented.
- A regular regional horizon-scanning brief that commercial and leadership teams rely on.
- No missed regulatory deadlines, and commercial teams that see Regulatory Affairs as a partner that helps them win.

What you bring

Essential

- A bachelor's degree in law, finance, economics, business or a related discipline.
- 4 to 6 years' experience in regulatory affairs, regulatory compliance or a related role in fintech, mobile money, microfinance, banking or telecoms.
- Working knowledge of the regulatory architecture and how BEAC, COBAC and national authorities work in practice, including the frameworks for payment services and microfinance.
- Direct experience engaging regulators, whether through licensing, inspections, consultations or reporting.
- Professional fluency in French and English, written and spoken, including drafting formal submissions in both.
- Strong analytical judgement: you can read a new regulation, work out what it means for the business and explain it clearly to non-specialists.
- Confidence to work independently, manage several markets at once and influence people without direct authority.

Nice to have

- Experience with digital lending, credit scoring or partnerships between MNOs and banks.
- Knowledge of AML/CFT, consumer protection and data protection requirements in key markets.
- A professional compliance qualification (for example ICA or CAMS) or a law degree.
- A network across regulators, industry associations or the fintech ecosystem in key markets
- A genuine passion for financial inclusion.
