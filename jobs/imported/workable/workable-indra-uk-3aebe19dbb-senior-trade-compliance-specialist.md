We are looking for an experienced Senior Trade Compliance Specialist to join Park Air and play a key role in supporting our international aviation and defence business.

This is a senior, hands-on position for someone with strong export control and trade compliance experience who enjoys working through complex issues and providing practical advice to the wider business.

As our international projects grow in size and complexity, we are seeing increasingly significant requirements around product classification, export licensing, sanctions, end-user and end-use due diligence, US-origin content and customer compliance obligations.

The successful candidate will provide specialist expertise across these areas and work closely with Commercial, Sales, Engineering, Supply Chain, Projects and Logistics to ensure trade compliance requirements are understood and addressed from the earliest stages of an opportunity through to final delivery and ongoing support.

UK strategic export controls can apply to goods, software and technology, including military and dual-use items, with exporters responsible for determining whether the appropriate export authorisation is required. UK strategic export controls

What You'll Be Doing

Your responsibilities will include:

- Providing expert advice on UK and international export control requirements.
- Undertaking military and dual-use classification of equipment, components, software and technology.
- Preparing and managing export licence applications and associated compliance requirements.
- Assessing US-origin content, including ECCNs, EAR99, de minimis and re-export considerations.
- Identifying and escalating potential ITAR-controlled content or technology.
- Conducting and reviewing sanctions, denied-party, end-user and end-use due diligence.
- Supporting enhanced due diligence for higher-risk destinations, customers and transactions.
- Reviewing End User Undertakings, End User Statements and supporting compliance documentation.
- Reviewing trade compliance and due diligence requirements flowed down through customer contracts and tenders, ensuring obligations are understood, achievable and appropriately flowed down to suppliers where required.
- Supporting major projects with significant or complex classification and licensing requirements.
- Advising the business on temporary exports, demonstrations, exhibitions, loan equipment, repairs and returns.
- Providing trade compliance input to bids, tenders and contract reviews so that potential restrictions are identified early.
- Maintaining clear and auditable records supporting classifications, licensing and due diligence decisions.
- Supporting the development of trade compliance procedures, processes, internal controls and training.
- Monitoring regulatory developments and helping the business understand their practical impact.
- Acting as a senior point of escalation for complex trade compliance questions.
- The Export Control Joint Unit administers the UK's export control and licensing system for military and dual-use items.

Requirements

What We're Looking For

We are looking for someone with significant practical experience in export controls or international trade compliance, ideally gained within the defence, aerospace, aviation, communications or another highly regulated technology environment.

You will have at least 5+ years experience in the following areas:

- UK Strategic Export Controls.
- Military and dual-use classification.
- Export licensing and licence management.
- Sanctions and restricted-party screening.
- End-user and end-use due diligence.
- US Export Administration Regulations (EAR).
- ECCNs, EAR99 and de minimis assessments.
- US re-export controls and an understanding of ITAR.
- Customs classification, origin and international shipping requirements.
- Trade compliance requirements within customer and supplier contracts.
- Working with government, defence or other sensitive end-users.
- Most importantly, you will be able to take complex regulatory requirements and turn them into clear, proportionate and practical advice for the business.

About You

You'll be someone who:

- Has strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
- Is comfortable making evidence-based compliance decisions.
- Can work independently on complex cases.
- Takes a pragmatic, risk-based approach to compliance.
- Is confident challenging or escalating a transaction when necessary.
- Communicates complex subjects clearly to non-specialists.
- Can work effectively across Commercial, Engineering, Supply Chain, Projects, Sales and Logistics.
- Is organised and comfortable managing multiple international projects and priorities.
- Understands that good trade compliance should enable legitimate business while protecting the company.
