## About Me

I turn certification requirements into automated, auditable controls-as-code.

🔐 WHAT I'M BUILDING

ONI, a GRC-as-code platform I designed and built: every client's compliance record lives as version-controlled YAML, every change to a control, evidence item, or gap goes through a reviewed pull request, CI blocks merges that overstate readiness, and no automated step can mark a control verified. It covers ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001, and SOC 2, with AI-assisted document intake that drafts gaps for a human to approve. I use it to deliver readiness engagements at Trustology.

🛡️ WHERE I COME FROM

Five years running a 24/7 regional security operations center for the Marine Corps Enterprise Network in the Pacific: 30 analysts across three shifts, 90,000+ endpoints, 700+ critical incidents resolved. I stood up its cyber threat intelligence cell (+37% proactive threat identification), cut false positives 44% with SIEM tuning, cut mean time to respond 27% by fixing the relationships across the response chain, and led RMF authorizations for the SOC's own tooling. The RNOSC received the Excellence in Federal Government Award twice while I was there. Seven years as an Army signal NCO came before that — Iraq, Okinawa, Fort Campbell — including DIACAP ATO packages for $2.4M secure VTC suites.

🎓 WHAT I STUDIED

My doctorate (DBA, Information Technology, 2026) examined how frontline SOC and NOC analysts actually adopt, trust, and work alongside AI. The finding that shaped everything I build since: AI works as an imperfect cognitive utility that needs human authority kept by design, not bolted on. That became the Aderhold A.R.C. framework — a 45-item diagnostic scoring 15 AI-adoption risk dimensions, mapped to NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 — and the human-in-the-loop rules at the core of ONI.
